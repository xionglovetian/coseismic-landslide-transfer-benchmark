# Decision on Optional All-Region Expansion

Date: 2026-09-25

Decision: defer and do not run the optional all-seven-region, two-architecture, two-budget expansion for the current Route 1 submission cycle.

## Rationale

- The Route 1 claim is already bounded to the tested exposure-matched package and does not require all-region replication for a tile-benchmark submission.
- The optional expansion would create a new estimand and substantially enlarge the analysis, code and reporting burden.
- It would not resolve the missing complete-map, independent-label or source-geolocation limitations.
- The current priority is to finish the existing evidence package, public release and manuscript consistency.

## Revisit Conditions

1. Reviewers explicitly require broader exposure-matched replication for the causal claim.
2. A target journal requires all coseismic regions and two architecture families for eligibility.
3. The authors choose a longer project timeline and allocate the required compute.
4. Source composition or budget interactions become a central scientific question rather than a secondary robustness check.

## Scope of Any Future Expansion

If revisited, use a new frozen protocol and run: two architecture families, three seeds, two matched exposure budgets, all six coseismic regions plus separate Longxi River reporting, and the same source-only single-stream, replay-placebo and pooled-source regimes.
